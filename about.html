Our Story

A Firm Built Around Client Trust

Arun Jamuna & Co. was established in August 2009 by CA Arun Kumar, a Fellow Member of The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, with a simple objective: to provide reliable, transparent and technically sound financial services that hold up under scrutiny. Today the firm is a partnership of four Chartered Accountants, supported by a team of more than 17 qualified professionals across our Head Office and branch offices, offering a wide range of advisory, assurance and representative services.

We are RBI-empanelled (Category II) and CAG-empanelled, and we hold a valid Peer Review Certificate — credentials that reflect our sustained track record in statutory, concurrent, stock & receivables and revenue audits of public sector and private banks across India, alongside our core accounting, taxation, GST and advisory practice.
