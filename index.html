About the Firm

Your Trusted Partner in Financial Success

Established in August 2009 by CA Arun Kumar, FCA, Arun Jamuna & Co. is a partnership of four Chartered Accountants providing audit, taxation, GST, accounting and advisory services from our Head Office in Navi Mumbai and branch offices in New Delhi and Thane. We are RBI-empanelled (Category II, UCN 956471) and CAG-empanelled (WR3995), with a valid Peer Review Certificate and a strong track record in bank audits alongside our core accounting and advisory practice.
