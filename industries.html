Industries We Serve

Our advice is shaped by the specific operational and regulatory realities of your sector.

Banking & Financial Institutions

Statutory, concurrent, stock & receivables and revenue audits for public sector and private banks — we are RBI-empanelled (Category II) with audit experience across 15+ banks nationwide.

Startups

Structuring, compliance, funding-readiness and financial systems for early-stage businesses.

Manufacturing

Cost accounting, inventory valuation, GST and compliance support for manufacturing units.

Trading

Accounting and taxation designed around trading cycles, margins and stock movement.

Inventory-aware bookkeeping, point-of-sale reconciliation and GST compliance.
