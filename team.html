Meet Our Team

Experienced professionals committed to giving every client clear, practical financial guidance.

CA Arun Kumar

Founding Partner

B.Com, FCA, DISA (ICAI), Certified Concurrent Auditor (ICAI), Certified in Forensic Accounting & Fraud Detection (ICAI), Certificate Course on GST, Certificate Course on Forex & Treasury Management

A Fellow Member of The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, CA Arun Kumar founded the firm in August 2009 and brings more than 19 years of experience in Bank Stock Audit, Concurrent Audit, Bank Statutory Audit and Revenue Audit, alongside Tax Audit, Internal Audit and audits of co-operative societies, PSU, government and autonomous bodies.

CA Aakriti Choudhary

Partner

B.Com, FCA

A Fellow Member of The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India with more than 9 years of experience in Bank Concurrent Audits, Stock Audits, Bank Statutory Audit and Revenue Audit, and as a financial advisor/consultant to corporate clients.

CA Randhir Singh

Partner

B.Com, LLB, FCA, Certified Concurrent Auditor (ICAI), Registered Valuer (IBBI)

A Fellow Member of The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India with more than 8 years of experience in Bank Stock Audit, Concurrent Audit, Bank Statutory Audit and Revenue Audit. Specializes in bank audits and business outsourcing operations, and advises corporate clients on financial matters.

CA Anita Amit Maurya

Partner

B.Com, FCA
